# Price Comparison

To make sure we are getting the most bang for the buck, I created a
[price comparison table](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1kJ4AdCbZYQlSQ2yOezcn3ApZPejCnOpOeinDMrVkBYM).
It lists the main parts (like ADC, DAC, coarse approach) for this and other DIY
SPM project. By now I didn't include AFMs, because they are so different from my
design that there's basically no comparison of parts.

Note that some projects are indicated with "Not all the parts". It may be
because they are not publicly specified, the project is already too expensive,
or just because I didn't bother searching enough.

I also want to point out that the graph alone makes the project of Philip Turner
not seem as competitive, but he is going for a super advanced design with
virtually no piezo distortions and compatible with ultra high vacuum, that may
futurelly allow manufacturing at the nanoscale. It's very cool to
[watch it unfold](https://x.com/philipturnerar), and it's extensively documented
([here](https://github.com/philipturner/capacitive-displacement-metrology) and
[here](https://github.com/philipturner/transimpedance-amplifier)).
